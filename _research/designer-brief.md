# App Elements — Designer Brief

**What the app is:** One app where a business owner runs a company staffed by AI employees. They can see
their team and its structure, manage the work, and talk with the AI employees.

**What we need from you:**
One organized **static demo of the whole app**, containing every element below — no logic, no
interactions needed. Arrangement, layout and visual style are your call.

---

## 1. Company / Team

- A list of AI employees
- For each employee: avatar, name, job title, department, status (working / idle / paused / error),
  what they are doing now, and a small cost or budget indicator
- The reporting structure shown as a hierarchy (who is above whom)
- Departments or teams
- One employee's full profile: details, skills, tasks, conversations
- A place to add a new employee

## 2. Tasks & Inbox

- A board with columns and cards
- A task card: title, owner (AI or person), priority, due date, progress
- Task detail: description, checklist, files, comments, activity history
- An inbox for items that need **my decision or approval** (approve / reject / ask)
- Notifications
- Filters and search

## 3. Conversations

- A list of conversations (one-to-one and group, mixing people and AI employees)
- A message thread: text, files, images, links, voice notes
- **Choice of AI model:** one or more models available, with a visible model selector inside the
  conversation
- Mentions of employees (@) and replies
- Message states: sending, sent, working (the AI is acting), failed, needs approval

## 4. The Visual Network — reserved

- Reserve a full area for a visual, zoomable view where the company, the tasks and the conversations
  appear connected as one network.
- **Do not design this part yet** — only reserve its place and the way to open it.

## 5. App-wide

- A way to move between the parts above (main navigation)
- Global search
- User account and company settings
- Dark and light appearance
- **Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR)** — the same screens in both
- Desktop first, but usable on mobile
- Empty, loading and error states for every list
- Accessibility: usable with a keyboard, readable contrast

---

*Reserved area note for the designer:* the visual network in section 4 is a fixed place in the app.
Its final look will be designed later with the team — leave it as an empty reserved area with a title.
